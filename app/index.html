    About this guide

    Leonida Ledger covers the 55 official PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver and 46 bronze, 28 of them hidden on PlayStation until earned. Every guide is written in our own words and names its sources — the official trophy list as listed by Exophase and PSNProfiles, and the published trophy guide and roadmap by PowerPyx, with Insider Gaming and GTASpotter where noted. No Rockstar logo or official artwork is used, and the app works with the network off: it loads only its own files.
